Prefer finishing started work over starting new work.

A task at review or inspect goes to the role that provides that stage
as soon as it is ready; do not hold it for a builder to finish
something else.

A role that holds a task is working on it: do not retry or stop it
unless its last attempt failed. Retry a failed task at once; a task
past its attempts is blocked with a reason, never retried again.

Push a role's branch once its attempt reports done.
